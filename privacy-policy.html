Privacy Policy — Habit Tracker-Kickoff

Last updated: 19 October 2026

Habit Tracker-Kickoff helps you build habits, keep streaks and write short journal notes. It has no account, no login and no server of its own. Everything you enter stays on your phone.

The app does show adverts. Adverts are the one part of the app that involves another company: Google AdMob serves them and collects some information about your device to do so. That is described in full below.

What the app stores on your device

In the app's private storage on your phone:

  • Your habits, schedules, reminders, check-ins and streaks
  • Journal notes, check-in answers, mood entries and focus sessions
  • The name you choose to show on the Today screen, your goals and rewards
  • Your settings, including an optional app-lock PIN, which is stored only as a salted hash and never in readable form

None of this is sent to the developer or to any server run by the developer. Other apps cannot read it. Uninstalling the app, or using Settings → Delete all data, removes it permanently.

Backups you control

  • Android backup. If you have turned on backup for your phone in Android settings, Android may include the app's habit database in your device backup to your Google account, so it can be restored on a new phone. This is handled by Android and Google, not by the developer. Your app-lock PIN and other settings are never included.
  • Export backup. Settings → Export backup creates a file in a place you choose. It is only shared if you share it yourself.
  • Share progress. The share card is an image created on your phone. It is only sent where you choose to send it, and private habits are left out.

Adverts, and what Google collects

The app is free and shows adverts supplied by Google AdMob:

  • Small banner adverts on some screens
  • An occasional full-screen advert after you finish a focus session or close the weekly review, at most once a day
  • An optional video advert you can choose to watch to earn a streak freeze. It only plays if you tap to watch it.

To select and measure adverts, Google may collect and process:

  • Your device's advertising ID, a resettable identifier used for advertising
  • IP address, and approximate location derived from it
  • Device information such as model, operating system version and language
  • Ad interaction data: that an advert was shown, and whether it was tapped

Google acts as an independent controller of this data. What it does with it is governed by Google's own policies, not by this one:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services

The developer does not receive any of this information. AdMob shows the developer only aggregate totals, such as how many adverts were shown and the earnings, never anything about an individual person. Your habits, notes and other app data are never shared with Google or any advertiser.

Your choices

Consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland). If you are in a region covered by the GDPR, the app asks for your consent before personalised adverts are served, using Google's User Messaging Platform. You can change or withdraw that choice at any time from Settings → Privacy options.

Everyone. You can limit ad tracking in Android settings: Settings → Google → Ads → Delete advertising ID (or Opt out of Ads Personalisation on older versions). This does not stop adverts; it stops them being personalised.

Permissions the app requests

  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS: to show the reminders you set up.
  • SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM: so reminders arrive at the exact time you chose rather than up to an hour late.
  • RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED: to restore your reminders after the phone restarts.
  • INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: used only by the advertising SDK to fetch adverts. The app itself makes no network requests.
  • com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID and Android's ACCESS_ADSERVICES_* permissions: access to the advertising ID and Android's privacy-preserving ad APIs, used only by the advertising SDK.
  • WAKE_LOCK and FOREGROUND_SERVICE: added by Android's WorkManager library so scheduled background tasks, such as the weekly summary, can finish.

Reminders are created and shown entirely on your phone. No reminder or notification content leaves the device.

What is never collected

  • No email address, phone number or account of any kind
  • No precise location
  • No contacts, photos, microphone or camera access
  • No analytics or crash-reporting SDK
  • Nothing at all by the developer directly. The only data collection is by Google's advertising SDK, as described above.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13. Because it serves advertising that uses a device advertising identifier, it is intended for a general audience aged 13 and over.

Data deletion

Delete your app data with Settings → Delete all data, or by uninstalling the app. To reset the advertising identifier held by Google, use the Android setting above. Requests about data Google holds should be directed to Google using their privacy policy.

Changes to this policy

If a future version changes what is collected, this policy will be updated before that version is published, and the date at the top will change.
